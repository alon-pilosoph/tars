A voice assistant for one household

TARS runs on a Raspberry Pi at home and uses a few cloud services for the hard parts. It answers in about 1.2 seconds, and nothing leaves the house until a second, stricter check is sure it heard “hey TARS”.

Things the household says to it

Alon Hey TARS, how long do I boil an egg?

Alon Lock the front door.

Alon Tell Stacey dinner's ready when she's back.

Alon Order the usual margherita from Wolt.

Aloud at home, or in a Telegram chat from anywhere.
How it worksOne request, second by second

What happens between “hey TARS” and the answer: the wake word checked twice on the Pi, the cloud opened only then, the answer started before you finish, and a second option for every part.
