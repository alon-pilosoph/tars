What happens when you say “hey TARS”

TARS is a voice assistant for one household. It runs on a Raspberry Pi at home and uses a few cloud services for the hard parts. This page follows one real request from the first word to the follow-up.

Time About 1.2 s from your last word to its first sound. It starts answering before you finish, so the work overlaps. Boundary Nothing leaves the house until a second, stricter check is sure it heard “hey TARS”. Until then the cloud lanes stay closed.

01 One request, second by second

0 is the moment you stop talking

Everything left of 0 happens while you are still speaking. That is why the answer comes so fast: by the time you finish, the connections are open, the speaker is known and the reply is already being drafted.

    02 How it decides it was called

    A fast guess, then a careful check

    Stage 1 is cheap enough to run on every 80 ms of sound, so it is allowed to be a little eager. Stage 2 only runs when stage 1 fires, and it is the one that opens the door.

    Every 80 ms A block of sound from the mic
    Stage 1 Wake model scores it microWakeWord, local, 69 KB. A score of 0.5 or more goes on.
    Close, below 0.5 Near-miss Logged, never answered. It may be a missed “hey TARS”.
    score 0.5 or more
    Stage 2, the double-check Vosk listens for a small grammar, and a learned layer decides Still on the Pi. Nothing has left the house yet.
    Answer The cloud opens. Step 3 on the timeline.
    Ask “Did you call me?” The reply decides.
    Ignore Stays quiet. Kept for Review.
    How well the generic models do

    Trained on synthetic and converted voices, never on a household member. These are the models every new install starts with. The clips are on Hugging Face .

    Held-out synthetic voices, quiet 97%
    Same voices with TV or people talking, 10–15 dB 87–97%
    The owner, whom it never heard, 30 takes 93%
    Lookalikes let through, like “hey cars” and “hey stars” 0–5%
    False answers per hour, an hour of TV and an hour of audiobooks 1 / 0 One on the TV hour, when it heard “hey darts”. Stage 1 alone fires about 6 times an hour on TV and 3 on audiobooks; stage 2 turns down all the others.
    03 It learns from the household

    Every wake teaches the next pair of models

    The generic models start out good on voices they have never heard. The loop makes them good on yours. People only take part in three places, all in the web app.

    1. 1

      Every wake is kept

      Its 3 seconds of audio, the scores and what followed. Close calls, the near-misses, too. All of it stays on the Pi; nothing leaves the house for learning.

    2. 2

      Most label themselves

      What happened next says whether a wake was real.

    3. 3
      People, in the web app

      People answer the rest in Review

      A few taps a day. Each unsure wake has its audio, what TARS heard, what happened, and TARS's guess highlighted.

    4. 4
      People, in the web app

      Voices are grouped automatically

      Naming one lets TARS greet that person by name.

    5. 5

      A bigger machine trains a new pair

      A new stage 1 and stage 2, trained on those wakes in about 20 to 25 minutes. Every fifth wake is kept aside for testing, and the new pair is tested end to end against the one in use.

    6. 6
      The gate

      It installs only if it is better for this house and no worse anywhere else

      Better means it answers more of the household's real wakes, or lets fewer through that weren't for TARS. If not, it asks. Once installed, the Pi switches within seconds, and the Models page can switch back.

      Other voices, quiet 94.4% 97.8% better
      Other voices, noise 83.5% 88.3% better

      Not installed. Better on other voices, but worse on two things that matter. That is the rule doing its job.

    04 When something breaks

    Every stage has a second option

    TARS fails soft, and when it can't recover it tells you in its own voice.

    Stage Normally If that fails
    End of turn Deepgram Flux Local rules
    Speech to text Flux OpenAI transcribes the same recording
    Brain Qwen on Cerebras OpenAI answers
    Voice Deepgram Aura-2 A spoken error line, made at startup
    Wake models The newest trained pair The pair it was installed with
    Mic or speaker The USB speakerphone TARS exits and the system restarts it
