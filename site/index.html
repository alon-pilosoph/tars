A voice assistant for one household

TARS runs on a Raspberry Pi at home and uses a few cloud services for the hard parts.

TimeAbout 1.2 sfrom your last word to its first sound. BoundaryNothing leaves the houseuntil a second, stricter check is sure it heard “hey TARS”.
